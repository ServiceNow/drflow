Hi Aisha,

Adding benefits guidance for the welcome packet/onboarding workflow:

• Benefits enrollment timing: Our preference is that new tenure-track faculty complete benefits enrollment during their first week. This aligns with plan eligibility timelines and helps avoid retroactive corrections.

• How they enroll / support available:
  – They should attend Faculty Orientation (Benefits segment covers medical/dental/vision, retirement, and deadlines).
  – We strongly recommend they schedule a 1:1 benefits consultation with HR Benefits during that first week (especially if they have dependents or are transferring coverage).

Putting it together, the “must do” HR/payroll actions to avoid pay/coverage delays are:
1) Complete direct deposit + tax withholding in the HR portal before arrival (so Payroll can run the first payroll cleanly).
2) Complete the I-9 in person at check-in with original documents (to ensure compliant employment verification).
3) Enroll in benefits in week one via Orientation + 1:1 consult (to keep coverage effective dates and deductions on schedule).
