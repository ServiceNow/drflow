# Tenure-Track Faculty Appointment Integration Guide

## Introduction

This document provides standardized guidance for integrating newly appointed tenure-track faculty into Eastport University operations across colleges and central administrative units. It establishes consistent expectations for coordinating employment actions, access provisioning, and initial academic participation. The guidance is intended for department administrators, college HR partners, and central offices supporting faculty transitions. Adherence ensures timely readiness for teaching, research, and service responsibilities.

## New Faculty Transition Procedures

As part of the onboarding workflow for newly hired tenure-track faculty, departments must first confirm the PI’s startup requirements and either route a Startup Fund Authorization (SFA-3) for approval or assign a default department index when startup funding is not applicable. Once the SFA-3 is approved or near-final, the onboarding case manager reviews the funding parameters and anticipated purchasing activity to determine the appropriate purchasing access path. When the SFA-3 reflects startup funding exceeding $250,000 or includes any specialized equipment acquisition, Procurement and P-Card training is mandatory and completion must be verified before purchasing roles are activated. If these conditions are not present, standard purchasing access may be enabled after the index is created, allowing routine ordering while maintaining internal controls. In all cases, the decision rationale and either training completion evidence or the standard access approval must be documented in the faculty onboarding case to support audit readiness and consistent compliance.

## Conclusion

Consistent application of these guidelines supports a seamless transition for new tenure-track faculty and reduces delays that affect instruction and research productivity. Coordinated execution across departmental, college, and central offices improves data accuracy, compliance posture, and service quality. Standardization also strengthens equity by providing a uniform onboarding experience across colleges. Units should review and follow this guidance to ensure timely faculty readiness and a positive first-year experience.
