Hi Elena,

Yes—please send the hire details when you have them. For workflow/timing, here’s what we follow for newly hired tenure-track faculty:

1) Pre-arrival (as soon as offer is signed)
- HR enters the hire into PeopleAdmin/HRIS (this is what triggers downstream account provisioning).
- Background check status verified (if applicable to the role).
- Department submits the “New Faculty Access & Space” form (office/lab, phone, keys, building access).

2) Identity + payroll compliance (30–14 days before start, earlier if possible)
- I-9 instructions sent; documents verified on/near first day per policy, but we collect the intent/appointment ahead of time.
- Direct deposit/tax forms set up in Employee Self Service.

3) Accounts + systems provisioning (typically 21–10 business days before start)
- NetID/email and DUO/MFA created after HRIS record is active.
- Required role-based access: Canvas/teaching tools, library resources, VPN, shared drives, and any engineering-specific systems (e.g., lab computing, CAD license groups) once department confirms needs.
- Sponsored Projects onboarding if they will submit proposals immediately (ERA, Cayuse, etc.).

4) First-week requirements
- New Faculty Orientation session registration.
- Compliance training assignments (COI disclosure, responsible conduct of research, export controls if applicable, lab safety as needed).

For this particular case: the new hire is an Assistant Professor (tenure-track) in the College of Engineering with an Aug 15 start date. The offer has been signed, and the chair has asked that all accounts/access be ready by the first day (they want the faculty member able to access email, VPN, proposal systems, and engineering lab resources immediately).

Given that expectation, we should aim to have HRIS entry completed no later than mid-July so Mark’s team can provision NetID/MFA and core access with enough lead time.
