# Faculty Appointment Administration Standards Manual

## Introduction

This manual provides standardized administrative guidance to support consistent faculty appointment practices across Eastport University. It aligns college-level operations with institutional policy, regulatory expectations, and internal control requirements. The guidance is intended for department administrators, college HR partners, and central offices that support academic units. Adherence promotes audit readiness, clear accountability, and a consistent faculty experience.

## Academic Policy and Governance Alignment

To support Eastport University’s academic governance cycle, Institutional Research compiles monthly and term dashboards that align enrollment, retention, and graduation performance with Board-approved targets. Analysts extract source tables from the SIS and the institutional data warehouse, then clean and reconcile term-by-term counts before calculating core KPIs including headcount, FTE, yield, retention by cohort, graduation rates, and DFW rates. Dashboard packets for deans and trustees include multi-year trend lines, variance-to-target commentary, and brief notes on policy-relevant drivers such as course sequencing and gateway bottlenecks. Each release is archived in the reporting repository with version control, and the team maintains documented data definitions and calculation logic to meet auditability and accreditation evidence requirements.

## Conclusion

Consistent administrative practices reduce risk and ensure equitable treatment across colleges and departments. Following these guidelines supports timely decision-making, clear documentation, and coordinated service delivery. Units should periodically review local procedures to remain aligned with institutional policy and external requirements. Questions or exceptions should be escalated through the appropriate governance and compliance channels.
