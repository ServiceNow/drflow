# Tenure-Track Faculty Start Guide

## Introduction

This guide standardizes how Eastport University coordinates start-of-appointment activities for newly hired tenure-track faculty across colleges. It is designed for use by graduate program coordinators, department administrators, and college HR partners to reduce delays and ensure consistent communications. The document consolidates key handoffs, required documentation touchpoints, and timing expectations from offer acceptance through the first semester. Use this as a shared reference when planning start dates, collecting forms, and tracking completion milestones.

## Appointment Initiation and Stakeholder Handoffs

Once the offer is executed, we open the onboarding workflow and confirm cross-functional handoffs (department, HR, ISSS, and research administration) to ensure all systems are ready for the Aug 15 start date. Because this hire will launch as a PI with a $300k startup allocation, the department and central finance should prioritize creating the associated index and enabling the appropriate procurement profiles. In practice, the unit routes the Startup Fund Authorization (SFA-3) and selects the “>$250k” funding tier so purchasing authority can be established without delaying lab and equipment acquisitions. We also set an ongoing compliance cadence that includes visa document tracking for the J-1 record and an annual COI attestation, anticipating sponsored research activity during year one.

## Compliance Documentation and Recordkeeping Standards

Eastport’s records management audit (completed 2024-11-22) found that 41% of departments stored compliance-related PDFs in shared drives without consistent version labels, increasing retrieval time during spot checks. The University Archives issued a standardized naming convention (e.g., UNIT_YYYYMMDD_DOC-TYPE_v##) and recommended quarterly retention reviews for folders exceeding 2,000 files. During the January 2025 cleanup, Engineering reduced redundant files by 13% and moved 486 items into the approved repository with updated metadata fields. A follow-up audit is scheduled for 2026-01-15 to assess adherence to the retention schedule and access controls.

## Orientation Schedule and Communication Templates

For Spring 2025, the Graduate School ran three campus-wide orientation events (01-08, 01-09, and 01-10) and achieved a 92% RSVP-to-attendance rate after switching to a two-reminder cadence. Email performance for the “Welcome Week” template set averaged a 48% open rate and a 12% click-through rate, with the highest engagement occurring on messages sent at 9:15 a.m. Central Communications also A/B tested subject lines across 6,400 recipients and observed a 9% lift when including the location (e.g., ‘Mason Hall’) in the subject. Survey responses (n=1,128) indicated that signage and parking instructions were the top two requested improvements for Fall 2025 planning.

## Conclusion

Following the defined handoffs and milestone tracking will help departments align HR, college offices, and program staff on a single start timeline. Prioritize early confirmation of start date, access needs, and required documentation to avoid downstream impacts to payroll, system provisioning, and teaching assignments. Program coordinators should use the communication templates to keep new faculty informed and to document completion status in the shared tracker. Next steps are to confirm responsible parties by college and publish the annual onboarding calendar with updated deadlines.
