# Tenure-Track Faculty Start-Up Administration Guide

## Introduction

This guide defines the administrative standards used across Eastport University to support successful faculty start-ups. It establishes role clarity and coordination points among Academic Affairs, colleges, departments, HR, Sponsored Programs, and central service units. The procedures apply to newly hired tenure-track faculty and related stakeholders involved in pre-arrival and first-term readiness. Consistent execution ensures compliance, timely access to resources, and an effective transition into teaching and research responsibilities.

## Hiring Authorization and Budget Controls

As part of hiring authorization controls, HR must validate work eligibility within the required timeframe while ensuring the immigration workflow remains aligned with ISSS case-type requirements before advancing any related actions. During onboarding case setup, confirm whether the new tenure-track faculty member can be physically on campus no later than day 3; record this determination and the selected verification approach in the onboarding case to maintain auditability. If the faculty member will be on site, schedule the I-9 document review during the initial check-in appointment and ensure the verification is completed and entered in the system in accordance with university policy. If the faculty member cannot appear by day 3, appoint an authorized remote verifier, issue standardized instructions and due dates, and obtain the verifier’s signed I9-R1 memo for filing in the onboarding case. This documented plan supports compliance with federal verification rules, prevents payroll or appointment delays, and provides the budget owner and dean’s office with confirmation that the hire is eligible to proceed under established controls.

## Conclusion

Adhering to these guidelines promotes a consistent faculty start-up experience across colleges and departments. Standardized practices reduce delays in access, payroll, research enablement, and instructional readiness while strengthening compliance. Clear handoffs and documentation improve accountability among participating units and service providers. Routine review and continuous improvement of these procedures help sustain Eastport University’s research and teaching mission as hiring scales.
