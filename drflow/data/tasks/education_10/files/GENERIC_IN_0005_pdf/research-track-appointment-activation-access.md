# New Faculty Appointment Activation Guide

## Introduction

This manual provides standardized guidance for initiating and coordinating key actions associated with new research-track faculty appointments at Eastport University. It is intended for use by college and department administrators, HR partners, and central offices supporting academic personnel. Consistent execution supports compliance, timely access to institutional resources, and a positive faculty experience across colleges.

## Appointment Activation and Access Setup

Before activating the appointment and provisioning institutional access, route PDF-7 through the correct approval pathway for the faculty member’s appointment type to ensure the hire is eligible to proceed in the onboarding case. Once approvals are verified, open the onboarding record in EastportWorks and review the Pre-Employment Checks area to determine whether a background screening step must be triggered. For benefits-eligible positions or roles with student contact, launch the BGC-2 authorization workflow and enter the hire’s identity details, hiring unit/department, and the appropriate screening package if the system prompts for it. For appointments that do not meet those criteria, document a clear waiver in the case notes and checklist, stating that a background check is not required and citing the rationale and eligibility criteria applied. Save the update and confirm the case checklist reflects the correct completion status so downstream activation, network access, and payroll setup can proceed without compliance holds.

## Conclusion

Adhering to these guidelines ensures consistent, auditable handling of academic appointments across the university. Clear role ownership and timely sequencing reduce delays in pay, access, and research readiness. Standard practices also support regulatory requirements and protect institutional data. Units should review and update local checklists to align with these procedures each hiring cycle.
