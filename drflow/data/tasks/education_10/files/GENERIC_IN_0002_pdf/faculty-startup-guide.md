# Tenure-Track Faculty Appointment Start-Up Guide

## Introduction

This document provides standardized guidance for coordinating key activities associated with appointing and integrating new tenure-track faculty at Eastport University. It aligns cross-functional responsibilities across academic units, central administration, and shared service offices to ensure a consistent faculty experience. The guidance supports timely activation of employment, research readiness, and access to essential university resources. Local college procedures may supplement this manual where noted.

## Appointment Activation and Start-Up Steps

After a new faculty onboarding case is opened in EastportWorks using the signed offer letter information, the department initiator must prepare the Faculty Onboarding Packet (FOP-1) to trigger appointment activation across administrative units. In the FOP-1 form, enter the faculty member’s identity and contact details, summarize the appointment and position parameters, and confirm the effective start date, home department, work location, and the designated supervisor or departmental point of contact. Include any required funding/index placeholders so Payroll and the dean’s office can validate pay distribution and budget alignment, and attach the signed offer letter if the form prompts for supporting documentation. Before submission, verify that routing recipients are correctly selected so HR, Payroll, IT, and the dean’s office receive the packet without manual follow-up. Submit the completed FOP-1 and document the submission confirmation (e.g., transaction ID or timestamp) in the EastportWorks case notes to support auditability and ensure downstream provisioning proceeds on schedule.

## Conclusion

Consistent execution of these guidelines reduces delays in faculty start dates, research ramp-up, and access to university systems. Clear handoffs and documented responsibilities help ensure regulatory compliance and accurate employment records. Adhering to the outlined practices supports a positive onboarding experience for new hires and their departments. Colleges should review this guidance annually to reflect policy and system changes.
