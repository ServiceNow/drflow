# Tenure-Track Faculty Appointment Guide

## Introduction

This document provides standardized guidelines for coordinating faculty appointment activities across Eastport University’s colleges and central administrative offices. It clarifies roles, required checkpoints, and documentation expectations to support consistent, compliant implementation at an R1 scale. The guidance applies to new tenure-track hires and the departments, colleges, and shared services that support them. Units should use this manual to align timelines and reduce delays that impact research, teaching, and payroll readiness.

## Pre-Arrival Compliance and Credentialing

Pre-arrival compliance and credentialing begins by establishing a formal onboarding record in EastportWorks based on the fully executed offer letter. HR staff should access the Onboarding/Case Management area and initiate a new faculty onboarding case, entering the faculty member’s legal name and, where prompted by policy, a personal email address to support secure pre-start communications. The signed offer letter must be uploaded to the case as a PDF to provide an auditable source document for appointment verification and downstream approvals. The proposed start date should be recorded to align I-9 timing, benefits eligibility, and onboarding milestones, and the correct home department/organizational unit must be selected to route tasks to the appropriate college and administrative partners. Staff must also designate the appointment category (e.g., tenure-track, research-track, or term) to ensure the correct compliance rulesets and provisioning workflows are applied. Saving the entry generates a unique case ID, which becomes the system-of-record reference used to track completion of pre-arrival checks, credentialing actions, and required clearances.

## Conclusion

Consistent adherence to these guidelines helps the University deliver a coordinated faculty experience while meeting policy, labor, and regulatory requirements. Clear ownership of tasks reduces administrative rework and improves readiness for teaching, research operations, and sponsored project activity. Standard documentation and checkpoints also strengthen auditability and equity across colleges. Units should review and update local procedures to remain aligned with this institutional guidance.
