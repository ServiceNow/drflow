# New Faculty Appointment Administration Manual

## Introduction

This manual establishes standardized administrative guidelines for integrating newly appointed tenure-track faculty across Eastport University’s colleges. It aligns academic affairs, human resources, and research administration practices to ensure consistent, timely service delivery. The document outlines governance expectations, required approvals, and supporting operations that enable new faculty to begin teaching and research without interruption. Units should apply these guidelines in coordination with college-level procedures and applicable collective bargaining or policy requirements.

## Appointment Processing and Start Readiness

Appointment processing begins only after the faculty hire has completed core payroll and identity prerequisites, including the W-4 (or equivalent tax form), direct deposit setup, and the New Hire Data Sheet (NHD-4). Once these are on file, the onboarding coordinator reviews the individual’s self-reported citizenship or permanent residency status captured in the onboarding intake/NHD-4 to determine the appropriate work-authorization pathway. For hires who are not confirmed as U.S. citizens or permanent residents, the coordinator initiates an ISSS/Designated School Official (DSO) case using the appointment and position details and requests the International Scholar Packet (IS-10) checklist to collect required documentation such as passport information, CV, degree verification, and prior immigration history. For hires who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents, the coordinator bypasses ISSS processing and schedules the standard I-9 document review appointment in accordance with university timelines. In all cases, the selected pathway and next steps are recorded in the onboarding case to ensure compliance visibility across HR, the hiring unit, and ISSS. This sequencing reduces start-date risk by aligning appointment readiness with both federal I-9 requirements and, where applicable, institutional immigration sponsorship protocols.

## Conclusion

Consistent execution of these guidelines reduces delays, improves the new faculty experience, and strengthens institutional accountability. Clear coordination across central offices and colleges supports rapid activation of payroll, systems access, and research operations. Documented steps and approvals help maintain policy compliance and audit readiness. Units should review this manual annually to reflect policy updates and operational lessons learned.
