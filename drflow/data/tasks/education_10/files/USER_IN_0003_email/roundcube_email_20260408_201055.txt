Hi Aisha,

For a new tenure-track faculty member who is a non-U.S. citizen and currently outside the U.S., our standard path is entry in J-1 “Professor” status.

Pre-start immigration steps (ISSS):
1) Department opens an ISSS case (this is required to initiate sponsorship).
2) For the DS-2019 request, the department uploads the required documents:
   • Passport biographic page
   • Current CV
   • Signed offer letter/appointment letter
3) Once ISSS issues the DS-2019, the scholar can proceed with the consular visa process and plan travel/start date accordingly.

Timing note: DS-2019 issuance/visa processing is often the longest lead-time item and can impact start readiness, so it’s best treated as a gating step in the onboarding workflow.

Best,
