# Tenure-Track Faculty Appointment Guidelines

## Introduction

This manual defines standard administrative practices supporting the successful integration of new tenure-track faculty at Eastport University. It aligns academic, HR, compliance, and IT activities to ensure consistent execution across colleges and departments. The guidance is intended for department chairs, college business officers, HR partners, and central administrative units. Adherence to these standards supports timely readiness for teaching, research, and service responsibilities.

## Faculty Appointment and Onboarding Procedures

After the IT Access Request (IT-8) and Campus Card Request (CCR-6) are submitted to enable system credentials and ID access, the department onboarding coordinator must confirm with the chair and hiring administrator whether the new tenure-track faculty member will function as a Principal Investigator (PI) and/or receive startup support. If PI designation and/or startup funding applies, the coordinator prepares the Startup Fund Authorization (SFA-3) with the approved amount, permitted expense categories, expected research or project deliverables, and any required speedtype/index setup to support purchasing and account creation. The SFA-3 must then be routed for review and signature through Sponsored Programs, Finance, and Procurement to ensure compliance with institutional and sponsor requirements before funds are released. If the appointment does not include PI activity or startup resources, the coordinator requests creation or assignment of the standard departmental index in lieu of an SFA-3. In all cases, the final index/speedtype decision and any approvals are documented in the onboarding case file to support downstream transactions, including lab setup, procurement, and financial reporting.

## Conclusion

Consistent application of these guidelines promotes an efficient, well-documented start for new faculty and reduces avoidable delays. Standardized coordination across units strengthens compliance, financial stewardship, and the faculty experience. Departments should use this manual as the baseline for local checklists and role assignments. Questions or exceptions should be routed through the designated college and central administrative contacts to ensure appropriate approvals.
