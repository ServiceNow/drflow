# Compliance Incident Management Standards Manual

## Introduction

This manual outlines HarborPoint Community Bank’s internal standards for managing potential compliance incidents in a consistent, well-controlled manner. It establishes governance expectations, documentation norms, and escalation pathways aligned to banking regulatory obligations. These guidelines support effective oversight across lending and treasury operations while protecting customer and employee confidentiality. Adherence is required for all business units and control functions involved in issue management.

## Incident Intake and Triage Controls

All potential internal compliance violations must be logged immediately as a formal matter to ensure consistent triage, evidence preservation, and auditability. The investigator or designated intake analyst opens the Compliance Case Portal (CCP), selects the option to create a New Case, and initiates Form CP-101 (Concern Intake) as the system-of-record for the allegation. During intake, the analyst records the reporting channel (e.g., hotline, email, or management escalation), identifies the impacted business unit, and defines the relevant date range for the suspected activity to guide scoping and retention queries. The CP-101 record must also capture every individual named (including full name and, when available, title and department), the systems implicated (such as core banking applications, payment platforms, or shared drives), and any transaction identifiers or reference numbers provided by the reporter. Once all required fields are completed, the form is saved and the user verifies that CCP has generated a unique case record, which triggers downstream triage controls and ensures the investigation proceeds under documented governance.

## Conclusion

Consistent execution of these standards helps HarborPoint detect and address compliance concerns with appropriate rigor and transparency. Clear documentation, controlled evidence handling, and timely escalation reduce regulatory, legal, and reputational exposure. Following this guidance also enables effective oversight by Compliance, Legal, Internal Audit, and business leadership. All personnel are expected to apply these procedures to support a strong culture of compliance.
