# HarborPoint Operational Governance Reference Guide

## Introduction

This document outlines core operational governance practices supporting HarborPoint Community Bank’s delivery of lending and treasury services to small and mid-sized businesses. It is intended to align day-to-day execution with enterprise risk management expectations and consistent customer outcomes. The guidance is designed for managers and frontline teams across business units and support functions. Adoption of these standards promotes clarity, accountability, and scalable operations.

## Enterprise Risk Ownership Model

Under HarborPoint’s enterprise risk ownership model, the Small Business Lending line owns the accuracy and timeliness of month-end pipeline reporting as a first-line responsibility, with Finance and Risk Analytics providing independent challenge on methodology. Each reporting cycle, the lending operations analyst refreshes the pipeline report by pulling current opportunity data from the loan origination system (LOS) and CRM, then reconciling stage definitions (application, underwriting, approval, closing) to ensure consistent governance of portfolio visibility. The analyst updates expected close dates and probability-weighted amounts, summarizes results by product type (term loan, line of credit, SBA), relationship manager, and industry segment, and publishes the updated forecast to the lending leadership dashboard. If mismatches appear between LOS and CRM, the analyst creates a reconciliation log and requests corrections from the assigned relationship managers; otherwise, the forecast is released as the official month-end view.

## Conclusion

Consistent governance practices help ensure HarborPoint operates with discipline across products, channels, and teams. Applying these guidelines supports reliable service delivery, clearer decision rights, and stronger documentation quality. Standardization also reduces operational variability as the organization scales. Leaders are expected to reinforce these expectations through ongoing oversight and periodic review.
