Hi Lena,

Yes—two procurement steps are already in motion specifically to reduce the backorders:

1) We renegotiated the blanket purchase agreement (BPA) with our primary office supplies vendor to lock in branch-priority fulfillment and pre-allocated inventory for the highest-volume SKUs (toner, pens, receipt paper, deposit slip envelopes). The vendor confirmed the BPA amendment went live last Friday.

2) We implemented a minimum-on-hand reorder threshold for high-use items in the centralized catalog. Instead of branches ordering ad hoc, the system now triggers replenishment once stock drops below the set minimums (by region), which should smooth demand spikes and prevent “empty shelf” periods.

This is a supply-chain/process improvement to improve availability and consistency—there’s no compliance or record-hold driver behind the change.

Eric is tracking the KPI impact (backorder rate and average fulfillment time) and can share the first weekly readout after Friday’s cycle.
