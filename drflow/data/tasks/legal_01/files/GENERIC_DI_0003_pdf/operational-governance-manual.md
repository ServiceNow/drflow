# HarborPoint Operational Governance Manual

## Introduction

This manual outlines the governance practices that support consistent, well-controlled operations across HarborPoint Community Bank. It is intended for leaders and staff involved in client-facing services, back-office execution, and risk functions. The guidance aligns with enterprise standards for documentation, approvals, and operational accountability. Adherence to these practices promotes reliability, transparency, and sound decision-making.

## Enterprise Risk and Control Framework

As part of HarborPoint Community Bank’s enterprise risk and control framework, IT performs quarterly access recertification for commercial online banking and treasury management platforms to evidence least-privilege controls. The IT governance team generates current internal user and role listings from the identity provider and each vendor’s admin console, then issues role-based access attestations to department managers for review. Managers submit approvals and any required changes, after which IT removes or adjusts access for terminated employees and staff whose job responsibilities have changed, and records completion status and exceptions in the IT governance tracker. If a manager does not respond by the deadline, IT escalates to the department head and temporarily restricts high-privilege access pending confirmation; otherwise, attestation records are finalized and retained for audit support.

## Conclusion

Consistent execution of these guidelines helps ensure HarborPoint operates with strong control discipline and clear accountability. Standardized practices reduce operational variability and support timely escalation when issues arise. Maintaining accurate records and well-defined roles strengthens oversight and audit readiness. All teams should apply this manual as a baseline for day-to-day operational governance.
