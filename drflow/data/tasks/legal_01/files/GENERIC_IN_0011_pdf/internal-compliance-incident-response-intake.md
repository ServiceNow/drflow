# Internal Compliance Incident Response Guidelines

## Introduction

This document provides HarborPoint Community Bank with standardized guidance for managing and documenting potential compliance concerns across lending and treasury operations. It establishes consistent internal expectations for information handling, stakeholder coordination, and control integrity. The guidance supports timely decision-making and defensible outcomes aligned with regulatory and audit requirements. These standards apply to all employees, contractors, and business units.

## Compliance Case Intake and Triage

After a case is logged and preliminarily scoped, the Investigator must first notify the appropriate specialist function (e.g., HR, Legal, BSA/AML, or Information Security) and complete any track-specific intake artifacts required for that allegation type before scheduling any discussions. Using the approved interview plan, identify required interviewees, confirm the sequencing to preserve independence and evidence integrity, and coordinate calendars without disclosing unnecessary details beyond a need-to-know basis. Where allegations involve workplace misconduct such as harassment, retaliation, or threats, interviews are coordinated through HR under the HR-22 workflow; otherwise, the team conducts compliance interviews using the CI-5 script to ensure consistent questioning and an explicit reminder of the Bank’s non-retaliation expectations. Interview logistics (date, time, participants, and location or conferencing details) must be recorded in CCP, with access restricted to the case team to maintain confidentiality. During each interview, the interviewer follows the designated process, captures contemporaneous notes, and obtains any required acknowledgments. All notes, signed forms, and related materials must be uploaded to the Evidence Vault using standardized naming conventions and accompanied by custody entries to support auditability and defensible investigative outcomes.

## Conclusion

Consistent execution of these guidelines helps HarborPoint identify issues early, preserve relevant records, and reduce regulatory and reputational risk. Clear governance and documentation support credible outcomes for management, auditors, and regulators. Adhering to defined roles and controls also protects customer information and maintains operational continuity. All personnel are expected to follow these standards and escalate uncertainties through established channels.
