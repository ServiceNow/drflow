# Compliance Incident Management Standards Manual

## Introduction

This manual establishes HarborPoint Community Bank’s internal standards for managing suspected compliance-related events. It aligns expectations across Lending, Treasury, Operations, and Corporate Compliance to ensure consistent handling and documentation. The guidance supports timely escalation, proper confidentiality, and preservation of relevant records. These standards apply to employees, contractors, and third-party service interactions where applicable.

## Compliance Event Intake and Triage

During intake and triage of a potential internal compliance violation, the investigator first schedules fact-finding interviews through approved channels using the standard script, including a non-retaliation reminder, before analysis begins. Once initial statements and artifacts are collected, the case is structured in CCP by mapping the applicable policy provisions to the specific control activities expected for the business line (e.g., lending documentation, treasury onboarding, or transaction monitoring). For each mapped requirement, the investigator records observed evidence from the Evidence Vault with direct file links, relevant excerpts, and timestamps to preserve an auditable trail. The triage assessment then classifies each control as supported, unsupported due to missing evidence, or deficient where execution clearly failed, and these determinations are logged as preliminary findings in CCP with clear cross-references. If evidence is incomplete or contradictory, the investigator flags targeted follow-ups and requests additional records before elevating the matter to a formal investigation, ensuring HarborPoint’s response is timely, consistent, and defensible.

## Conclusion

Consistent application of these standards reduces regulatory, legal, and reputational risk for HarborPoint. Clear intake, documentation, and confidentiality practices enable accurate fact-finding and appropriate escalation. Following the guidelines supports defensible outcomes and preserves stakeholder trust. All business units are expected to execute their responsibilities promptly and in accordance with established controls.
