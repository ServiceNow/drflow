Danielle,

Thanks for flagging. We’ve been tracking similar reports and have two reliability improvements in flight that should address most of what you’re seeing:

1) Standardized meeting kit: IT is rolling out a bank-standard headset + webcam kit (same models/config across users) to reduce variability from consumer-grade devices/drivers that tend to cause echo, mic dropouts, and camera freezes.

2) Increased default bandwidth allocation: We increased the default bandwidth allocation for our video conferencing service on the network side (QoS/policy) so video traffic is less likely to be throttled during peak utilization, particularly for remote users on VPN.

Neither of these changes touches security monitoring, evidence retention, AML controls, or investigative workflows—this is purely end-user enablement to make remote meetings more stable.
