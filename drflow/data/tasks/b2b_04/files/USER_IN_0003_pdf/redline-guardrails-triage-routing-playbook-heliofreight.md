# Redline Governance for High-Velocity Deals

## Introduction

This internal guide summarizes how HelioFreight Commercial Counsel and Deal Desk partners manage contract redlines within established legal guardrails. The intent is to standardize intake, review pathways, and escalation criteria so sales teams can maintain momentum without increasing risk. It is designed for account executives, sales operations, pricing, and regional leadership supporting customer negotiations. The document aligns contract review practices with our 3PL operating model, liability profile, and approval authorities.

## Redline Intake, Triage, and Routing

The CLM team completed a compare-tool upgrade on 02/14/2026 that improved document ingestion accuracy from 93% to 97% across tracked file types (DOCX, PDF, and mixed-format scans). Over the last 90 days, 28% of submissions included at least one non-editable attachment, prompting a broader initiative with Sales Ops to standardize file naming conventions and metadata tagging by region. Legal Operations also piloted a dashboard showing average clause-level deviation counts per counterparty, with the Midwest warehousing segment averaging 6.2 deviations per agreement versus 4.7 in the Northeast. These platform and reporting changes are aimed at data quality and forecasting rather than directing how approvals are executed.

## Contract Risk Tiers and Authority Limits

Our authority matrix assigns approval paths based on commercial value and operational complexity to keep redlines moving without unnecessary executive gates. This opportunity is projected at approximately $3.2M in annualized revenue and, because it does not require a dedicated buildout or the launch of a new facility, it remains within the standard routing tier. As a result, the workflow should proceed through the normal Legal, Finance, and Operations approvers, without escalating to a VP Operations checkpoint tied to value/buildout thresholds. Confirming the deal fits this tier upfront reduces cycle time by preventing avoidable re-routing once clause deviations (e.g., liability or indemnity) are identified as higher-risk items for Legal review.

## Customer Communication and Record Retention

Customer-facing communications are standardized through approved email templates maintained by Brand and Legal, with the latest refresh published on 01/08/2026 and adopted by 19 commercial teams. Record retention is governed by the enterprise policy that requires executed agreements to be retained for 7 years post-termination, and we completed a retention audit on 03/22/2026 covering 120 sampled contracts with a 96% pass rate. For key accounts, account managers often request a short commercial recap to share internally, but those summaries are treated as non-binding and stored separately from the executed MSA and SOW package. These practices support consistency and defensibility in disputes but do not address how approvals are obtained.

## Conclusion

Consistent risk tiering, clear authority limits, and disciplined routing are the core levers for reducing turnaround time on redlines while protecting HelioFreight’s position. Teams should use the intake and triage pathway to avoid ad hoc reviews and ensure high-impact issues are escalated only when needed. Commercial Counsel will maintain the risk tiers and preferred fallback language based on claims trends and customer negotiation patterns. Next steps are to reinforce adoption through Deal Desk training and to monitor cycle times and exception rates quarterly.
