[2026-04-04 21:55:00] #commercial-legal:redline-summary-rss14
@maria.chen:
Fastest way is to drive everything through the Redline Summary Sheet (RSS-14). I’m preparing it now.

Process we use to approve redlines quickly:
1) I extract each customer deviation (by clause) into RSS-14.
2) For each deviation, Legal proposes (a) our preferred response and (b) fallback language we can live with.
3) We tag the business/risk owner for any residual risk (e.g., Finance for liability cap exceptions, Ops for SLA/service credit mechanics) and get their explicit sign-off in the sheet.
4) Once RSS-14 is complete, we post it in #deal-approvals for a single-thread decision instead of multiple reviewers re-reading the full redline.
5) After owners approve the residual risk + fallback positions, Priya can confirm we’re within guardrails and we move to signature-ready docs.

This reduces cycle time because approvers can decide off the deltas + named risk owner, not re-litigate the whole agreement.
------------------------------------------------------------
